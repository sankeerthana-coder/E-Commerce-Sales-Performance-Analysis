\# E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis



\## 📌 Project Overview



This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Python and Pandas to understand sales performance, customer behavior, regional trends, delivery performance, expenses, and profitability.



The project includes data exploration, data cleaning, data transformation, statistical analysis, and business insights.



\## 🎯 Objectives



\- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.

\- Calculate total revenue and expenses.

\- Analyze revenue by product category and region.

\- Identify top-performing products and customers.

\- Analyze monthly sales trends.

\- Analyze payment methods used by customers.

\- Evaluate delivery performance and customer ratings.

\- Calculate profit and loss.

\- Identify profitable categories and orders.



\## 🛠️ Technologies Used



\- Python

\- Pandas

\- Jupyter Notebook



\## 📂 Dataset



The dataset contains e-commerce order information with the following fields:



\- `order\_id` – Unique order identifier

\- `order\_date` – Date of the order

\- `customer\_id` – Unique customer identifier

\- `product\_category` – Product category

\- `region` – Customer/order region

\- `quantity` – Number of products purchased

\- `unit\_price` – Selling price per unit

\- `discount` – Discount applied to the order

\- `payment\_method` – Payment method used

\- `delivery\_days` – Number of days taken for delivery

\- `customer\_rating` – Customer rating

\- `revenue` – Revenue generated

\- `Year` – Extracted year from order date

\- `month` – Extracted month

\- `Month\_name` – Extracted month name

\- `cost\_price` – Cost price per unit

\- `Expenses` – Total expenses

\- `Profit\_Loss` – Profit or loss from the order
